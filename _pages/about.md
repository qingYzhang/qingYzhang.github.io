I’m Steven Zhang, a second-year master’s student in Information Networking at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/). I study multimodal learning and large language models for healthcare, with a focus on representations that generalize across clinical datasets.

I currently work with Prof. [Sumit Chopra](https://www.spchopra.net/) at NYU Grossman School of Medicine on MRI reconstruction using the fastMRI brain dataset.

Previously, I worked with Prof. [Chenyan Xiong](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~cx/) at CMU on cross-national EHR representation learning and domain adaptation. During my undergraduate studies at [New York University](https://www.nyu.edu/), I worked with Prof. [Sumit Chopra](https://www.spchopra.net/) and Prof. [Yiqiu Shen](https://seyiqi.github.io/) on automated radiology report generation and clinical information extraction.

## Selected research

### [Bridging the EHR Divide](/publication/2026-12-10-bridging-the-ehr-divide)

*NeurIPS 2026 GenAI4Health Workshop · Accepted, poster*

An asymmetric contrastive learning objective for transferring medical representations from Taiwan’s longitudinal health records to U.S. clinical datasets.

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04946) · [Code](https://github.com/qingYzhang/Asymmetric_SupCon)

### [BURExtract-Llama](/publication/2024-10-31-burextract-llama)

*MCHM 2024 · Multimedia Computing for Health and Medicine Workshop*

A fine-tuned Llama 3 model that extracts structured clinical information from breast ultrasound reports for lower-cost, in-house deployment.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3688868.3689200) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.11334)
