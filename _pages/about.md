I’m Steven Zhang, a second-year master’s student in Information Networking at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/). I study multimodal learning and large language models for healthcare, with a focus on representations that generalize across clinical datasets.

I currently work with Prof. [Sumit Chopra](https://www.spchopra.net/) at NYU Grossman School of Medicine on MRI reconstruction using the fastMRI brain dataset.

Previously, I worked with Prof. [Chenyan Xiong](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~cx/) at CMU on cross-national EHR representation learning and domain adaptation. During my undergraduate studies at [New York University](https://www.nyu.edu/), I worked with Prof. Sumit Chopra and Prof. [Yiqiu Shen](https://seyiqi.github.io/) on automated radiology report generation and clinical information extraction.
